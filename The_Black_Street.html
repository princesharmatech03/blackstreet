About us

A neighbourhood café built around good company and good food.

Our story

The Black Street started with a simple idea: a place where nobody is in a hurry. Long tables, comfortable banquettes and a mural of an old European street give the room the feel of a lane you’d happily get lost in.

The kitchen cooks North Indian favourites the way they’re meant to be eaten: slow-simmered gravies, fresh paneer, fragrant spices and bread straight from the tandoor. Plates are made for sharing, so order a few and pass them round.

What we care about

Three things we try to get right every day.

Cooked fresh

Room to linger
